THE APPROACH IN CONTEXT

Imagine a
family retailer.

Customers aren't coming back as often as expected. A conventional response might be another campaign. Our first instinct is to ask: what else is connected to this outcome?

01 / OBSERVE

Start with what is known.

Map repeat visits, service experience, and available customer signals.

02 / CONNECT

Look beyond one cause.

Explore wait times, expectations, staff routines, and follow-up.

03 / TEST

Change one meaningful thing.

Trial a practical fix with a clear owner, timeline, and measure.

04 / LEARN

Let evidence guide the next move.

Review results and limitations before deciding what to scale.

Illustrative scenario only. Not a client project, reported result, or performance claim.
